Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · Effective: October 26, 2026

Code Crafted Nexus LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, disclose, and safeguard personal information through BoardSmyth AI and our website at boardsmythai.com.

1. Data Controller

Code Crafted Nexus LLC, a Kentucky limited liability company, is the data controller for personal data processed through BoardSmyth AI. Contact: support@boardsmythai.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Information: Your email address, encrypted authentication tokens, and user ID handled via our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Billing & Subscription Information: Subscription status, billing tier, renewal dates, and transaction identifiers. All payment card details and billing addresses are collected and processed directly by our merchant of record, Paddle. We do not collect or store full credit card numbers.
  • Prompts & Project Inputs: On the AI-Included Plan, text prompts, your current code, target board selections, any photos you add (for example a picture of your wiring), and any serial-monitor text you choose to send with "Ask AI about this" are transmitted through our backend infrastructure to our AI model provider (Anthropic) to generate the requested code or answer. Our servers pass this content through to the provider and do not keep it; we keep only a count of how many requests you make.
  • Phone-Camera Photos: If you use "Photo from phone," the app shows a QR code with a random one-time session code. Your phone sends the photo to our backend under that code, and the app collects it. The photo is deleted from our servers as soon as the app collects it, and any photo that is not collected is deleted automatically within one hour. We do not use these photos for any other purpose.
  • Terms Acceptance Record: When you tick "I agree" (or accept updated terms in the app), we store the version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted and the date and time, attached to your account. Our authentication provider may also record your IP address in its security logs.
  • Usage Information: Monthly generation counts, used to enforce subscription tiers.
  • Local Application Data: On the Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Plan, your Anthropic API key is stored locally on your device in your operating system's secure keychain/credential storage. Prompts and code originate directly from your desktop client to Anthropic's API endpoints and do not pass through or remain on our application servers. Your project sketches, board toolchains, serial monitor streams, and local source files remain on your local machine unless you choose to send part of them to the AI (for example by pressing "Ask AI about this" or adding a photo), in which case that content goes to the AI provider as described above.

How your content is used: we do not use your prompts, code, photos, serial-monitor text, or the AI's output to train machine-learning models. Under its commercial terms, our AI provider, Anthropic, does not use content submitted through its API to train its models. Anthropic's own privacy practices are described in its privacy policy.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom (UK), we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To provide access to BoardSmyth AI, manage subscriptions, process AI code requests, and furnish customer support.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): To prevent fraudulent usage, secure our API endpoints, enforce tier limits, and improve service stability.
  • Legal Compliance (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): To maintain necessary tax, accounting, and compliance records alongside our merchant of record.
  • Contract Formation and Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR): To keep a record that you accepted our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, so we can show the agreement we have with you.

4. Third-Party Service Providers (Sub-Processors)

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We disclose information strictly to service providers who assist in operating the Service:

  • Paddle: Merchant of Record, payment processing, sales tax, VAT, and invoice compliance (UK/US).
  • Supabase: Cloud database hosting, user account authentication, session handling, and the temporary relay for phone-camera photos (US/EU).
  • Anthropic, PBC: Large language model provider powering code generation and answers (US). On the AI-Included Plan, content goes to Anthropic through our backend; on the BYOK Plan, it goes directly from your device to Anthropic using your own key.
  • Transactional Email Provider: Delivery of password resets, system alerts, and support communications.

International transfers from the EEA/UK to the United States are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decisions where applicable.

5. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account remains active. Generation count metrics are retained for the duration of the monthly billing cycle and historical billing validation. Phone-camera photos are deleted when the app collects them and in any event within one hour. We do not store your prompts, code, or the AI's responses on our servers. Your record of accepting the Terms is kept for as long as your account exists and for a reasonable period afterwards to show that you agreed. You may request account deletion at any time by emailing support@boardsmythai.com.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, UK, and California / US States)

Depending on your location, you have statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we maintain about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal account data.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: EEA/UK residents have the right to lodge a complaint with their local Data Protection Authority.

To exercise any right, contact support@boardsmythai.com. We respond to verified requests within statutory timeframes (30 days).
